## Verdict

## Risks and open questions

- **Test injection of `PhotoLibraryClient`.** Island props are JSON-only; the current `client` prop pattern cannot survive `createIslandTest`. A module-level override or a thin non-island test wrapper is needed; decide before rewriting 801 lines of tests.
- **Selection state across cards.** Reactive `checked` is rejected; `modelChecked` needs a writable signal per card. Choosing `Map<string, Signal<boolean>>` vs remount-by-key changes how `Select all`/`Clear selection` (`App.tsx:490, 497`) are implemented.
- **Panel re-fetch on photo change.** Component props are snapshot at setup; either remount `RelatedPhotosPanel` through a keyed `For` or pass the photo as a signal. Remounting loses the panel's scroll position and focus (acceptable — the current code refocuses on `photo.id` change anyway).
- **Reset differences.** `owned-v1` is not byte-for-byte preflight; the measured deltas (control backgrounds, `::placeholder`, `::file-selector-button`, `small`, `sub/sup`) must be checked on the search form and the sr-only file input.
- **Breakpoint semantics.** `wide` becomes a fixed 1088 px query; users with a non-16 px root font size now get the sticky layout at a different rem width than before.
- **`min-h-32` / `shadow-sm`** may be no-ops in the current build (no `@theme` entries); confirm before encoding them as tokens.
- **Audit noise.** `src/worker/**` and `*.test.*` live under `src/` and are scanned; only auditInfo today, but `wind.sources.exclude` (**next zfb release**, #3367) is the proper fix.
- **Docs timeline.** zudo-doc 6.0.0 has no release date; its integration floor is blocked on the next zfb release. Until then `apps/docs` stays on 5.x and keeps deploying.
- **Shallow clone.** Liveness of individual files (last-touched dates) could not be measured; the repo as a whole was last merged 2026-08-12.
